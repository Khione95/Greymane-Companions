BLOCKER - the H1 'character' check drops every comrade:
- `R8(R64(target+0x88)+1) != 1` keeps only kind 1, and kind 1 is Player.
- The kind enum at 0x191640 is: 0 User, 1 Player, 3 NonPlayerCharacter, 4 Mercenary, 5 Vehicle, 6 Pet, 7 Gimmick.
- Evidence:
  - 0x2152C50 requires kind 1 plus [+0x96] for the leader.

  - 0x2B47473 checks kind 3 and then the characterinfo index.
- A camp actor is kind 3; a summoned copy is likely kind 4. So every Recruit press is silently ignored.
- Fix: accept kind 3 or 4, or drop the check and rely on the pump's roster and clan-record checks.

HIGH - a race if the pump reuses Adopt / PHASE_START:
- Today Ask (SetType 1) runs START_MS = 500 ms after registration, behind a 250 ms pump. The log shows a 720 ms gap.
