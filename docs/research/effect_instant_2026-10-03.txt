===== effect
SUMMARY: The green particles are not made by code, a buff or an effect table. They belong to an animation action in the character's action chart, and her AI chart plays that action during its own teleport.

How it fits together:
- Effect files: effect/binary__/releasebin/fx_character_etc__teleport1.pae, teleport2.pae and teleport3.pae. All three are made of green oak-leaf particles (textures cdfx_bg_leaf_oak01a_ksh_*) with light and spark emitters.
- Which action plays them: TELEPORT_START, TELEPORT_ING and TELEPORT_END. For comrades these are in upperaction/2_mon/common_defaultupper.paac, which their NHM_Upper / NHW_Upper charts include; all three names point to one action node, 0x715. Damiane and Oongka use the player chart 1_pc/1_phm/basic_upper.paac (nodes 0x129, 0x12A, 0x12B).
- Who plays them: the CommonTeleportPackage of the shared AI chart 0xA17A8F3E (this copy is package #0x358).
  1. State InitLogic picks a branch from the variable _teleportType.
  2. TeleportStart plays TELEPORT_START (the burst where she stands).
  3. Teleport plays TELEPORT_ING and runs the TeleportDesc. The client sends TrAITeleportReq 0x82B and the server moves her (0x29841B0 -> 0x2AC5BF0).
  4. On OnTeleportSuccess, TeleportSuccess plays TELEPORT_END (the burst where she lands), then she goes back through AIBranch_CheckPoint#0.
  5. A second state, CommonTeleport, does the same move with no action, so no effect.
- Why the plugin's teleport has no effect: the raw move through ServerAi vt+0x208 plus the resync 0x2BCCC00 never enters this package, and the resync restarts her chart (0x206EED0).
- How vanilla catch-up starts for comrades: CommonMovePackage, state MovePath, OnUpdate. If _moveType == MercenaryOwnerFormationChase and the distance check passes, she runs CommonTeleportPackage with _teleportType = ToMercenaryOwner. She lands at a random spot around her mercenary owner (the player).

What the plugin can do:
- Option (a) works. The chart answers two events at chart level, and no package or state overrides them in any of the 34 charts that have them:
  - **0x51F2D258** (no name in the exe): sets _teleportType = ToLeader and starts CommonTeleportPackage. That leads to TeleportStart, so she gets the same green out/in look. Her destination is ToProcessTarget with target type 0x36 FormationPosition.
  - **0x0243AF2B OnTeleportBehindCamera**: sets _teleportType = BehindCamera, whose first branch is TeleportSpecialStart (TELEPORT_SPECIAL_* actions). Comrades' charts do not have those actions, so do not use it for comrades.
  - The plugin's existing RaiseEvent (ServerAi vt+0x188) forwards the event to her client as 0x8AA. For a client-run AI it does nothing else on the server (0x105CA588 only sends the packet).
- Option (b) is not workable: none of the 1116 packet types plays an effect on an actor. StartActionAck 0xBAF exists, but its action-info layout is not decoded and her own client AI would fight it.
- Option (c) is not workable: no buff or effectinfo row carries this effect.

Nothing was run or written in the game folder; I only read the plugin's own log there. My decode scripts and data are in scratchpad\gca\fx (chartlib.py plus .pkl files). They decode the pai object pools (states, containers, branches, value sets, compare values, destination descs, action descs).

- TeleportStart, on OnActionEnd_Upper: ForceRideOff if she is mounted, then Teleport.

ACTION REVIEW: Overall: no crash, wrong-thread or save risk, but it is unlikely to deliver the visible effect for comrades. Ship it only as a logged experiment with a fast fallback, not as the catch-up.

1. EVENT_TELEPORT_TO_LEADER 0x51F2D258. The data path is right, and the chart is shared by all roster comrades and by Damiane/Oongka. The main risk is ToLeader itself. Vanilla uses ToLeader only under LeaderFormationChase/PetFormationChase (Or#466) and gives comrades (MercenaryOwnerFormationChase) ToMercenaryOwner instead. ToLeader's destination is FormationPosition, resolved in protected code. In both logs every status line shows the comrade's leader key ([[a+0x68]+0x118]+0x1C) as 0, and the earlier research found that AccompanyMercenary gives 'no party, no leader'. The likely result is OnSetDestinationFail twice, then TeleportSuccess in place 96+ m away. That is a 5 s later catch-up with nothing gained.

Two more problems:
- Comrades' TELEPORT_START, TELEPORT_ING and TELEPORT_END are all one node, 0x715 in common_defaultupper. Nothing shows that node plays the leaf effect, so even a successful teleport may show no green effect.
- Max's log (no request while 64-121 m away for ~7 s, probe installed) shows the comrade's own CommonTeleportPackage path already failing before the request, for a reason not yet found. It may block ToLeader too.

2. Optional BehindCamera for Damiane/Oongka: drop it.
- The plugin does not manage them, and they already catch up by themselves.
- They can be the played character: the log shows switches to 000004/000006. Raising AI events on the player's own actor or on vanilla party AI is unverified.

3. Calling RaiseEvent from CatchUp: safe. It is the same builder sequence as Follow, on the server thread in the dispatch pump with no locks, calling (not hooking) vt+0x188. res 1/0 only means 'forwarded'.

4. vanillaTpSince marker: good. Extend it to every path that can Resync, teleport or re-raise Follow during the window:
- the manual Bring back key (game.cpp ~1891, BringBack);
- Dismiss Resync (~1802);
- Recover/LOST handling.
Or have those paths clear the marker first. After the fallback, log her flags. The client hide (0x8000000 via 0x919BE0) is not proven to clear on a chart restart, although earlier BringBacks did leave comrades visible.
