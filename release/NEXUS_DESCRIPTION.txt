[size=5][b]Greymane Companions[/b][/size]

Recruit the Greymanes from your camp as companions. They follow you through the world, fight at your side, ride with you, and go back to camp when you dismiss them.

[b]Features[/b]
• Walk up to a Greymane in your camp, press Ctrl for more talk options and choose Recruit
• Up to 6 Greymanes at once, besides Damiane and Oongka
• They drop what they were doing and follow you at once
• They fight with you, and kneel when they are beaten, as Damiane and Oongka do
• Left far behind, they catch up with the same green-leaves teleport as Damiane and Oongka
• When you ride, they ride with you on horses of their own, and get off when you do
• Shown on the map and the minimap
• Dismiss: he walks back to what he was doing (one brought to you after a fast travel stays and leaves when you are out of sight)
• Bring back (F10): your companions come to you
• A notice tells you who joined, or that your party is full
• Works best with Party HUD - their health on screen

[b]Settings[/b] (bin64\GreymaneCompanions.ini, written on the first start)
• BringBack = F10 - your companions come to you (Ctrl+, Shift+, Alt+ combinations work)
• TeleportEffect, HideRoutine, StayBehind, MapIcon, Horses, HorseSpawn - each feature can be turned off (1 on, 0 off)
• MapIconStyle = 0 - the party icon (1: the character icon with the name)

[b]Install[/b]
• Needs Crimson Desert Mod Manager (DMM) 3.x
• Drop the archive into DMM, enable it and apply
• DMM puts GreymaneCompanions.asi into bin64 and installs an ASI loader if there is none

[b]Notes[/b]
• The Greymanes you can recruit are the camp comrades who joined you through the scout missions
• Their horses are made by the mod; they do not whistle for them, and they get onto them at once rather than walking up to them
• Dismiss your Greymane companions before you uninstall the mod (the party list is part of your save)

[b]Compatibility[/b]
• Works with Character Creator, Seasons of Pywel and Party HUD
• HDR (RenoDX and others) is supported for the notices
